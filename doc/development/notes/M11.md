# M11 — Anomaly classifier (E)

**Status:** ✅ done 2026-10-22, on branch `develop`.

## What was built

### `spm/core/classifier.py` (pure)

- **`ClassificationContext`:** the plan's fields. `shift_candidates` and `active_shops_in_area` have defaults.
- **`ShiftEvidence(shop_id, recent_median, baseline_median, baseline_mad, recent_prices)`.**
- **`build_shift_evidence(obs_by_shop, now, *, recent_h=48, baseline_d=30)`:** per shop, the median of the last 48 h
  against the median and MAD of the 30 days before that. Unweighted, included labels only.
- **`market_window_h(half_life_h)`:** `max(3 d, 2 × half-life)` in hours.
- **`classify(ctx) -> Assessment`** runs the plan's steps in order:
  1. Weights: `report_weight` inside the weighted centers.
  2. **Relative bounds:** with at least 5 area reports in 90 days, a price below 0.2× or above 5× the median → `BAD_REPORT`. The reference is the 90-day median.
  3. **`z_shop`:** with at least 4 history points, `robust_z` against the weighted median/MAD.
  4. **`z_market`:** with at least 5 market points in the market window, `robust_z` against the weighted median/MAD.
  5. **Market shift:**
     - The report's price is added to its own shop's recent set.
     - A shop counts when \|z(recent vs baseline)\| > 3 in the report's direction.
     - It needs at least `max(3, ceil(0.5 × active shops))` such shops → `MARKET_SHIFT`.
     - The reference is the median of those shops' baselines.
  6. **\|z_market\| > 3:** if corroborated (an independent agreeing report at the same shop, or a high-trust
     reporter) → `SHOP_OUTLIER`, otherwise `PENDING`. The reference is the market center.
  7. **No market view and \|z_shop\| > 3** → `PENDING`. The reference is the shop center.
  8. **Otherwise `NORMAL`,** with `insufficient_data` = no z at all.

  Every decision fills `z_shop` and `z_market` when they were computed, plus `reference_price`, `direction` (except
  NORMAL) and a readable `reason`.
- **Defensive filtering:** `classify` re-applies the label and window rules to its inputs (see Deviations).

## Deviations (also in the Decision log)

- **`ShiftEvidence` carries `recent_prices`,** and the baseline is the 30 days *before* the 48 h window.
- **Shift direction falls back to the market center** when the report's shop has no baseline.
- **`classify` re-filters its inputs,** and unscored observations aren't evidence.
- **`insufficient_data` is set only for NORMAL,** and zero-weight centers fall back to unweighted.
- **`market_window_h` is exported** for M13.

## Known limitations

- **Unit-suite timing is close to the limit.** In this module's verification it ran in 11.2 s, then 9.4 s and 6.7 s
  on re-runs; the only change between runs was machine load. §2.8 asks for under 10 s, so a busy machine can now push
  it over. If that becomes persistent, lower `max_examples` for the slowest Hypothesis tests (in `test_stats.py`)
  under a CI profile.
- **Only PENDING decisions are made here.** Resolving them later (to SHOP_OUTLIER, BAD_REPORT or EXPIRED) is the
  scoring worker's job in M13, using M10's `resolve_pending`, plus M17 for expiry.
- **Market-shift baselines are unweighted shop medians.** A shop with very few reports in the last 48 h can count as
  moved on one or two reports. The two-sided requirement (≥ 3 shops and ≥ 50% of active shops) limits the effect.
